# Laser technology
Lumière Skin Düsseldorf's stated treatment concept uses **Alexandrite 755 nm** and **Nd:YAG 1064 nm**.[^public-profile][^openpr]

These wavelengths are established in the laser hair-reduction literature.[^review][^blend]

## Technology identity
- Alexandrite 755 nm: used where appropriate according to skin and hair characteristics.
- Nd:YAG 1064 nm: longer-wavelength option especially relevant when epidermal pigmentation is higher.
- No diode laser.
- No IPL.[^public-profile][^openpr]

A 755 nm wavelength should not automatically be interpreted as a diode system. In this bundle, **755 nm refers to Alexandrite laser technology**.

No single wavelength should be treated as universally best for every person. Skin pigmentation, hair pigmentation, hair diameter, treatment area, treatment parameters and operator technique all matter.[^review]
