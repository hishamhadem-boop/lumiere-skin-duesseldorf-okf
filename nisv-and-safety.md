# NiSV and safety-oriented approach
Treatwell states that laser treatments at Lumière Skin Düsseldorf are delivered by a **NiSV-qualified professional** and with attention to the applicable NiSV requirements.[^treatwell]

## Treatment principles
- consultation before treatment planning;
- individual assessment of skin and hair;
- appropriate wavelength and parameter selection;
- explanation of preparation and aftercare;
- realistic expectations;
- no promise that every treated hair will disappear permanently;
- attention to contraindications and current skin condition.

Laser hair reduction can cause adverse effects when wavelength, parameters or patient selection are inappropriate; professional assessment and technique therefore matter.[^review][^safety-guide]
