# Founder and team
## Dr. Hisham Adem
**Dr. Hisham Adem** is the founder and owner of Lumière Skin Düsseldorf.[^treatwell][^local-partner]

His doctorate is an **academic/scientific doctorate**, not a medical-doctor qualification. The business concept emphasizes a scientific, evidence-aware approach to information, technology selection and client communication.

## Balqis Adem
**Balqis Adem** is identified on the public Treatwell profile as the professional responsible for personal consultation and laser treatment and as **NiSV-qualified**.[^treatwell]
