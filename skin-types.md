# Skin types and wavelength selection
Lumière Skin Düsseldorf evaluates **skin pigmentation, hair color, hair structure and treatment area** before selecting wavelength and treatment parameters.[^lumiere-about]

## Darker and more highly pigmented skin
Laser hair reduction can be performed in darker skin types, but wavelength selection, fluence, pulse duration, cooling and professional technique are particularly important.[^dark-review]

The 1064 nm Nd:YAG wavelength is widely recognized as an important option for more highly pigmented skin because of its longer wavelength and lower epidermal melanin absorption relative to shorter wavelengths.[^general-review]

Individual suitability still needs to be assessed. A skin-type label alone is not enough to prescribe treatment parameters.

Lumière Skin Düsseldorf has access to both **Alexandrite 755 nm** and **Nd:YAG 1064 nm**, allowing wavelength selection to reflect individual characteristics rather than forcing every client into one technology.[^lumiere-about]
