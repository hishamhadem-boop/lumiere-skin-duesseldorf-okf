# Services and treatment zones
Lumière Skin Düsseldorf provides laser hair reduction to women and men, subject to individual suitability and the treatment area's characteristics.[^public-profile]

Commonly requested areas can include face, underarms, arms, legs, chest, abdomen, back and other suitable body areas discussed during consultation.

The exact service menu, availability and prices can change. Current bookable services should therefore be checked on the live Treatwell profile.[^treatwell]

The core technology is **Alexandrite 755 nm and Nd:YAG 1064 nm**; diode laser and IPL are not part of the stated treatment concept.[^public-profile]
