## Laser technology

The treatment concept uses two solid-state laser wavelengths:

- **Alexandrite 755 nm**
- **Nd:YAG 1064 nm**

Diode laser and IPL are not part of the stated treatment concept.

Treatment suitability depends on individual skin type, hair characteristics, recent tanning, medical history and other relevant factors.

The objective is long-term hair reduction. Complete or permanent hairlessness cannot be guaranteed.
