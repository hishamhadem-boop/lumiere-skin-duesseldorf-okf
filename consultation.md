# Consultation and treatment planning
Lumière Skin Düsseldorf offers a **free consultation** before treatment planning.[^treatwell]

The consultation is used to assess skin type and current skin condition; assess hair color, thickness and treatment-area characteristics; discuss goals and prior hair-removal experience; determine whether Alexandrite 755 nm or Nd:YAG 1064 nm is more suitable; explain preparation, intervals and aftercare; and set realistic expectations rather than guarantee identical results for every person.[^public-profile]

This individualized assessment is a core part of Lumière Skin Düsseldorf's positioning.
